Design Document: Supply & Demand Market MVP

1. Goal & Concept
A console-based, agent-based economic simulation demonstrating price discovery and market equilibrium.

* Goods: Single commodity (Rockets).
* Agents: Buyers and Sellers with private valuations and dynamically updating price expectations.
* Mechanism: Random Search with Price Sampling & Midpoint Bargaining.

2. Configuration (config.json)
External setup parameters to control market scales and constraints:

* num_sellers: 10
* num_buyers: 10
* seller_min_range: [20, 40]
* buyer_max_range: [50, 80]
* sample_size: 5
* daily_yield: 1
* price_step: 2.0
* simulation_days: 300

3. Data Entities & State

Seller:

* id: Unique identifier.
* min_price (MIN): Private cost threshold / minimum acceptable selling price.
* expected_price: Current asking price. Initializes to MIN.
* inventory: Current stock available to sell.
* active: Boolean. Set to False if expected_price < min_price.

Buyer:

* id: Unique identifier.
* max_price (MAX): Private valuation / maximum budget limit.
* expected_price: Current target buying price. Initializes to MAX.
* inventory: Total rockets acquired.

4. Market Mechanics & Rules

A. Sales rule:
Simulation assumes "storefront" model, an transaction happens at the sellers expected price given its lower than buyer's expected price



B. Surplus Calculation:

* Producer Surplus = Transaction Price - MIN
* Consumer Surplus = MAX - Transaction Price
* Total Trade Surplus = MAX - MIN

C. Price Expectation Updates (End of Day):

* Successful Trade:
* Seller: Increases expected_price by price_step (perceives high demand).
* Buyer: Decreases expected_price by price_step (perceives lower costs).


* Unsuccessful Trade / No Sale:
* Seller: Decreases expected_price by price_step (tries to attract buyers).
* Buyer: Increases expected_price by price_step (realizes market is expensive).



5. Daily Simulation Loop (Tick)

FOR day = 1 TO simulation_days:
1. RESET / YIELD INVENTORY:
- Restore Seller inventory to daily_yield.

```
2. SHUFFLE BUYERS:
   - Randomize order of buyers to prevent bias.

3. MATCHING PHASE:
   FOR each buyer IN buyers:
       - Filter active sellers with inventory > 0.
       - Sample up to sample_size random sellers.
       - FOR each sampled seller:
           - IF seller.expected_price <= buyer.max_price AND buyer.expected_price >= seller.min_price:
               * Execute Trade at (MIN + MAX) / 2
               * Update inventories
               * Record trade statistics
               * BREAK loop (Buyer exits market for the day)

4. EXPECTATION ADJUSTMENT:
   - Update expected_price for all agents based on trade outcomes.
   - Deactivate sellers whose expected_price drops below MIN.

5. LOGGING:
   - Output daily summary to Console / Aggregator.

```
